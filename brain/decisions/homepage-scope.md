# `Process` stays off the homepage

**Decision.** The five-step process narrative lives at `/process` only.

**Why.** The audit flagged (M8) that the homepage has no process narrative. True,
but the fix is a judgement call, not an obligation. The homepage is already 13
sections and 15.5 viewport heights, all of it doing work. A pinned horizontal
track would add roughly another 1.5 screens to tell a story that is one nav click
away, and it would put a second pin on a page that already has one.

**Revisit if.** Analytics show readers leaving the homepage without reaching the
CTA, or the studio wants the method to be part of the first-visit pitch. The
component is built, responsive and ready to drop in.

## Related: what else was cut from the homepage

- **The second `SignalStrip`.** Repeating the page's one hard cut halves its
  force. It appears once.
- **The standalone `Newsletter` band.** It sat between the CTA and the footer and
  broke the closing movement. It now lives inside the footer's ink field.
